🍋 Privacy Policy for "It's a Lemon"

Last updated: October 25, 2026

"It's a Lemon" is a simple entertainment app for iPhone and iPad. This policy is short because the app does almost nothing behind the scenes.

  • No account or sign-up of any kind.
  • We collect no personal data. The game itself runs entirely on your device.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.
  • The game works without an internet connection. The only thing that goes online is "More games" (see below).

The app plays sound using on-device audio synthesis, which runs entirely on your phone and never leaves it.

More games. When you open "More games", the app downloads a short list of our other games and their icons from sharkedup.dev, and if you open one it loads that game's App Store page from Apple; these are ordinary web requests that send no account, name or other information about you, and we don't use them to identify or track you.

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.

Questions? Contact sharkedup@icloud.com.
